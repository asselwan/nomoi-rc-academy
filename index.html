Why this exists

Most RCM training is either a generic coding bootcamp or a vendor's product demo. This is neither.

Built from the work, not a curriculum vendor

Every module comes from patterns seen across years of real hospital billing operations: which denial reasons repeat, which coding gaps get caught late, which submission defects cost the most time. Examples in the lessons are illustrative and built to teach the pattern, not drawn from any patient or hospital record.

Built for the Saudi and GCC market

Coding rules, payer behavior, and submission standards differ by country. This course is written for teams working against NPHIES and GCC payer requirements, not a generic US-centric curriculum with the currency swapped.

One concept per lesson, checked, not skimmed

Each module ends with a worked exercise using clearly labeled sample data. You apply the framework before moving on, instead of watching a video and hoping it sticks.

A visible path, not a video library

The course tracks where you are against your own progress: lessons completed, exercises passed, modules remaining. There is no leaderboard and nothing compares you to other learners.
