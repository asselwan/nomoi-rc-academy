# Module 2 source notes

The lesson is an instructional synthesis of founder RCM material under `/home/ainur/Apps/.ainur`. No source records, patient identifiers, hospital identifiers, outcomes, or prices are reproduced in the public lesson. `EX-201` through `EX-204` are constructed exercise IDs.

- `RCM_PACKS/ELIG.md` and `RCM_PACKS/AUTH.md` distinguish eligibility and authorization denial families, while reporting that usable recovery amounts are unavailable. The lesson therefore treats front-end verification as a checkpoint and makes no recovery claim.
- `RCM_PACKS/CLAI.md` explicitly says its ClaimAct documentation rule is post-denial and pre-submission use is unverified. The lesson distinguishes pre-submission review from a payer denial rather than presenting a detector as a preventive guarantee.
- `RCM_PREADJ_JEV_LOOP_CHARTER_2026_09_23.md` separates original submission from first remittance and requires submission to precede remittance. The lesson uses those as distinct evidence checkpoints.
- `RCM_BRIEF_SOURCE_GROUNDING_2026_09_01.md` flags filing windows and resubmission-type claims as uncorroborated. The lesson gives no payer-specific deadline, code, or appeal instruction and directs learners to current payer terms.

The course remains a static learning artifact. A completed exercise records progress in the learner's browser only.
